Welcome to The Sunless Vault.

How to play

Move / attack: tap the board: you step toward the tap

Wait a turn: tap yourself (with the axe: swing it)

Descend / new run: tap the screen

Drink potion: the ! button

Use bomb: the * button (hits all around you, not you)

Weapon details: tap the weapon

Move / attack: WASD or Arrow keys

Wait a turn: Space (with the axe: swing it)

Descend / new run: Enter

Drink potion: P, or the ! button

Use bomb: B, or the * button (hits all around you, not you)

Weapon details: I, or click the weapon

New game: R   Help: ? or H

Walk into an enemy to attack it. Damage is fixed: red pips show enemy HP. Find the stairs (>) to descend, and reach the Vault below floor 10.

Gold tiles show where your weapon reaches; bright gold = what your attack would hit now. Gold HP pips = damage you'd deal (all gold: it dies).

Step on items to pick them up. Weapons swap: sword / hits 1 tile; spear ↑ hits 2 in a line, and stepping toward an enemy 2 tiles away strikes it; axe Y hits all 8 around (−1 damage), and waiting swings it; hammer T knocks back.

Kick a barrel O by walking into it: it rolls until it hits something and explodes (3×3). The shaded path shows where; red means you're in the blast. A key ⚷ opens a locked door +.

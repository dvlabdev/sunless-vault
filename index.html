Welcome to The Sunless Vault.

How to play

Move / attack: WASD or Arrow keys, or tap the board

Wait a turn: Space, or tap yourself

Descend / confirm: Enter, or tap

Drink potion: P, or the ! button

Use bomb: B, or the * button (hits all around you, not you)

New game: R

Help: ? or H

Walk into an enemy to attack it. Damage is fixed: red pips show enemy HP. Find the stairs (>) to descend, and reach the Vault below floor 10.

Step on items to pick them up. Weapons swap: spear ↑ hits 2 in a line, axe Y hits all around (−1 damage), hammer T knocks back. Walk into a barrel O to push it; blasts and impacts make it explode. A key ⚷ opens a locked door +.
